Project information


  • Role:
    Branding and Marketing Designer
  • Overview:
    The objective of this project was to create a comprehensive branding and marketing campaign for "Team Moose," an esports team associated with a popular YouTuber boasting over 10 million subscribers. The primary goal was to attract sponsorship deals by showcasing the team's value and engaging with the target audience of passionate gaming enthusiasts.

Uniforms

I crafted unique and eye-catching uniform designs that incorporated the team's logo and vibrant team colors, capturing the essence of their identity and ensuring they stood out during competitive matches.

Website

I developed a sleek and user-friendly single page website for Team Moose, optimized for mobile devices. The website featured an immersive design with seamless navigation, providing fans and potential sponsors with easy access to team information, match schedules, news updates, and a visually appealing browsing experience.

Marketing Deck

As part of the campaign, I created a comprehensive marketing deck that showcased the value proposition of Team Moose to potential sponsors. The deck highlighted the team's impressive achievements, social media reach, and the demographics of their passionate fan base. Through visually compelling slides and persuasive messaging, the marketing deck effectively communicated the immense exposure and brand visibility sponsors could expect by partnering with Team Moose.
